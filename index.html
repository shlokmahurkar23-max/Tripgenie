🔐 Payment safety

  • Only pay through a verified provider checkout or the property's official booking channel.
  • Review the total, taxes, cancellation and refund terms before authorising payment.
  • Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, password or card CVV with a person or TripGenie.
  • Don't treat a saved enquiry as a confirmed booking.
